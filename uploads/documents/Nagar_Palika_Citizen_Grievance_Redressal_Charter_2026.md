# NAGAR PALIKA CITIZEN GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL CHARTER 2026
**Document ID:** DOC-ADM-2026-001  
**Authority:** Department of Municipal Administration & Public Grievances  
**Effective Date:** 01 January 2026  
**Classification:** PUBLIC (CITIZEN ACCESS AUTHORIZED)

---page-break---

## SECTION 1: OVERVIEW AND COMPLAINT SUBMISSION PROCEDURE

1.1 All residents residing within municipal limits possess a statutory right to prompt, accountable civic service delivery under the Nagar Connect e-Governance Framework.
1.2 Grievances may be submitted 24x7 via the Nagar Connect Mobile Application, Citizen Web Portal, or designated Ward Civic Centers.
1.3 Upon lodging any grievance, a unique tracking identifier format `NGC-YYYY-XXXXXX` (e.g., `NGC-2026-000001`) shall be automatically generated and transmitted to the registered citizen mobile number and email.
1.4 Citizens are requested to attach geo-tagged photographs, landmark descriptions, and accurate ward identifiers to expedite field inspections.

---page-break---

## SECTION 2: STATUTORY SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA) TIMELINES

2.1 Every municipal complaint is bound by legally mandated resolution deadlines. Departmental officers and field engineers are evaluated based on their SLA compliance rates.

* **Severe Road Pothole / Crater:** 48 hours for temporary cold-mix patch; 7 days for complete hot-mix resurfacing.
* **Burst Water Main / Drinking Water Pipe Leakage:** 12 to 24 hours depending on main feeder diameter.
* **Contaminated Water Supply:** 12 hours emergency response; immediate deployment of alternate potable water tankers.
* **Uncollected Street Garbage / Solid Waste:** 24 hours from time of citizen registration.
* **Overflowing Community Dustbin:** 16 hours turnaround by sanitary vehicle tippers.
* **Non-functioning Streetlights / Dark Corridors:** 24 hours for single luminaires; 12 hours for series electrical feeder failures.
* **Blocked Stormwater Drain / Waterlogging:** 24 hours for minor desilting; 36 hours for major culvert clearance.
* **Dangerous Overhanging Tree Branch:** 48 hours tree pruning crew mobilization.

## SECTION 3: ESCALATION HIERARCHY AND GRIEVANCE APPEALS

3.1 If a grievance is not redressed within the specified SLA deadline, the automated system triggers an **Escalation Alert**:
* **Level 1 Escalation:** Automatically routed to the concerned Assistant Executive Engineer or Senior Health Officer with priority override.
* **Level 2 Escalation (SLA + 24 Hours):** Escalated directly to the Additional Municipal Commissioner.
* **Level 3 Escalation (SLA + 48 Hours):** Placed on the Municipal Commissioner’s Executive Review Dashboard for punitive audit.

3.2 Citizens reserve the right to reject an unsatisfactory resolution within 7 days of closure. Rejected complaints reopen at escalated priority with mandatory inspection by a gazetted supervisor.

---page-break---

## SECTION 4: CITIZEN VERIFICATION AND REDRESSAL SIGN-OFF

4.1 No complaint shall be classified as 'RESOLVED' solely on field staff testimony. 
4.2 Mandatory photographic evidence (Before and After completion) must be verified by the Department Nodal Officer.
4.3 The citizen receives an SMS/Portal notification containing the resolution summary and photographic evidence, with options to rate satisfaction from 1 to 5 stars.
