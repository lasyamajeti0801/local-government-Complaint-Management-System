# EMERGENCY CIVIC CONTINGENCY & EXECUTIVE ESCALATION PROTOCOL
**Document ID:** DOC-ADM-2026-006  
**Authority:** Office of the Municipal Commissioner  
**Effective Date:** 01 March 2026  
**Classification:** ADMIN_ONLY (COMMISSIONER, MUNICIPAL ADMIN & KNOWLEDGE ADMIN ONLY)

---page-break---

## SECTION 1: INUNDATION HOTSPOT EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS & DEWATERING

1.1 In the event of heavy monsoon precipitation exceeding 65 mm/hr, the 24x7 Municipal Disaster Cell automatically shifts to Emergency Level-3 Alert.
1.2 Zonal Joint Commissioners are empowered to commandeer diesel dewatering pumps of capacity 100 HP to 250 HP without prior tender concurrence under Section 89 of the Disaster Management Act.
1.3 Underpass water levels exceeding 45 cm mandate instantaneous road closure with physical police barricading and automated Nagar Connect civic broadcast alerts.

---page-break---

## SECTION 2: INTER-AGENCY WAR ROOM COORDINATION

2.1 The Municipal War Room operates in direct liaison with State Disaster Response Force (SDRF), Police Traffic Command, Fire Services, and Electricity DISCOMs.
2.2 Emergency electrical feeder isolations in submerged colonies shall be authorized exclusively by the Commissioner or Additional Commissioner to prevent electrocution casualties.
2.3 Daily evening briefing bulletins are issued to accredited press agencies exclusively by the Municipal Public Relations Officer.

---page-break---

## SECTION 3: EMERGENCY CONTINGENCY FUND EXPENDITURE LIMITS

3.1 Zonal Commissioners possess discretionary emergency sanction limits up to ₹ 15,00,000 per incident for emergency levee fortification, debris clearance, and temporary relief camps.
3.2 All financial sanctions executed under emergency provisions must be submitted for post-facto audit ratification to the Standing Municipal Committee within 30 days of incident cessation.
