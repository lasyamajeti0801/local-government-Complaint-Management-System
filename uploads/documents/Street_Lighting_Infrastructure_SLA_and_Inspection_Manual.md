## SECTION 1: ILLUMINATION BENCHMARKS AND LUX STANDARDS

1.1 Public street illumination levels must strictly adhere to the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Code SP:72:
* **Major Arterial Roads / Ring Roads:** Minimum 30 Lux, Uniformity ratio >= 0.40.
* **Secondary Collector Streets:** Minimum 15 Lux.
* **Residential Inner Colonies:** Minimum 8 Lux to 10 Lux.
* **Pedestrian Crosswalks & Junctions:** 40 Lux with focused high-mast optical luminaires.

---page-break---

## SECTION 2: SMART CCMS FEEDER PILLAR AND PHASE FAILURE DIAGNOSIS

2.1 All electrical feeders are connected to Centralized Control and Monitoring Systems (CCMS) with GSM telemetry reporting hourly current draws.
2.2 If current draw falls below 70% of nominal threshold between 06:30 PM and 06:00 AM, the CCMS automatically creates an automated Nagar Connect internal ticket.
2.3 When investigating dark corridors:
* Inspect 3-phase incoming MCB / MCCB breaker.
* Check neutral grounding resistance (must not exceed 5 Ohms).
* Test underground armoring continuity using digital insulation resistance (Megger) meter.

## SECTION 3: 24-HOUR RESTORATION SLA AND PENALTY CLAUSES

3.1 Individual luminaire failures must be restored within 24 hours of citizen complaint creation.
3.2 Entire lane / feeder circuit outages must be restored within 12 hours.
3.3 Annual Maintenance Concessionaire contract entails a deduction of ₹ 250 per pole per day of delayed restoration beyond SLA window.
