# POTHOLE REPAIR, COLD-MIX ASPHALT & MONSOON FIELD SAFETY SOP
**Document ID:** DOC-RDS-2026-005  
**Authority:** Department of Roads & Civil Infrastructure  
**Effective Date:** 20 February 2026  
**Classification:** FIELD_STAFF (FIELD ENGINEERS & GROUND TEAMS)

---page-break---

## SECTION 1: TRAFFIC CORDONING AND PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)

1.1 Prior to commencing any road repair activity, the field crew must secure the work perimeter:
* Deploy fluorescent orange retro-reflective traffic cones at least 30 meters ahead of the work zone on single-lane roads, and 50 meters on arterial roads.
* Every member of the field team must wear high-visibility Class-3 reflective safety vests, steel-toed work boots, and industrial gloves.
* Station one designated flagman equipped with a handheld stop/slow paddle during high-density daytime traffic.

---page-break---

## SECTION 2: EXCAVATION, EDGE CUTTING, AND DEBRIS EVACUATION

2.1 Do NOT dump asphalt mixture into an irregular or wet pothole.
2.2 Mark a neat rectangular perimeter around the distress area extending 50 mm beyond fractured road margins.
2.3 Chisel or diamond-saw cut the edges vertically to form a sharp, upright 90-degree boundary to provide lateral mechanical interlock.
2.4 Evacuate all standing water, loose aggregate, and mud using mechanical compressed air blowers or heavy-duty wire brooms until cavity base is dry.

---page-break---

## SECTION 3: TACK COAT APPLICATION AND POLYMER COLD-MIX COMPACTION

3.1 Spray an even application of cationic slow-setting bituminous emulsion (RS-1 or SS-1 tack coat) at 0.25 kg/m² across cavity bottom and cut vertical edges.
3.2 Lay polymer-modified cold-mix asphalt in uniform layers not exceeding 50 mm thickness.
3.3 Compact thoroughly using a mechanical vibratory plate compactor (minimum 10 kN compaction force) or light pedestrian roller making at least 6 overlapping passes.
3.4 Finish surface proud by 5 mm above surrounding asphalt level to allow for traffic kneading post-opening.

---page-break---

## SECTION 4: EVIDENCE CAPTURE PROTOCOL

4.1 Field staff must capture three mandatory photos using the Nagar Connect Field Staff Portal:
* **Photo 1 (Before):** Wide-angle view showing pothole with measuring tape or traffic cone for scale.
* **Photo 2 (During):** Rectangular edge-cut cavity with tack coat applied.
* **Photo 3 (After):** Fully compacted, clean asphalt surface opened to traffic.
