# SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT AND SANITATION BYLAWS 2026
**Document ID:** DOC-SAN-2026-002  
**Authority:** Department of Health, Sanitation & Solid Waste Management  
**Effective Date:** 15 January 2026  
**Classification:** PUBLIC (CITIZEN ACCESS AUTHORIZED)

---page-break---

## SECTION 1: SOURCE SEGREGATION OF MUNICIPAL SOLID WASTE

1.1 All domestic households, commercial establishments, institutions, and restaurants are legally mandated to segregate municipal waste at the source into four distinct streams:
* **Green Bin (Wet / Biodegradable Waste):** Food remains, fruit peels, leftover organic waste, garden leaves.
* **Blue Bin (Dry / Recyclable Waste):** Paper, plastic containers, dry packaging, cardboard, glass bottles, metal cans.
* **Red / Black Bin (Domestic Hazardous Waste):** Broken thermometers, paint cans, discarded medicines, aerosol sprays, batteries.
* **White Pouch (Sanitary Waste):** Diapers, sanitary napkins, biomedical bandages (must be wrapped securely).

1.2 Sanitary workers are instructed and empowered to decline collection of unsegregated, mixed waste.

## SECTION 2: DOOR-TO-DOOR COLLECTION PROTOCOL AND VEHICLE TRACKING

2.1 Door-to-door waste collection operates daily between 06:30 AM and 11:30 AM across all residential wards.
2.2 Every collection auto-tipper vehicle is integrated with GPS telemetry monitored at the central Municipal Command Center.
2.3 Commercial establishments generating more than 50 kg of waste per day are designated as **Bulk Waste Generators (BWGs)** and must institute on-site composting or contract certified municipal concessionaires.

## SECTION 3: PENALTIES FOR LITTERING, ILLEGAL DUMPING, AND OPEN BURNING

3.1 Open dumping of solid waste on public roads, footpaths, stormwater drains, or empty plots is strictly prohibited under Section 248 of the Municipalities Act.
3.2 Compounding fines are levied as follows:
* **First Offense (Residential Littering):** ₹ 500 spot fine issued by Sanitary Inspector.
* **Second Offense (Residential Littering):** ₹ 1,500 fine with mandatory sanitation warning.
* **Commercial Dumping on Pavements:** ₹ 5,000 spot fine for retail shops; ₹ 25,000 for hotels or banquet halls.
* **Open Burning of Plastic or Garbage:** ₹ 5,000 spot penalty under National Green Tribunal (NGT) air quality regulations.
* **Persistent Violations:** Cancellation of municipal trade license and civil prosecution.
