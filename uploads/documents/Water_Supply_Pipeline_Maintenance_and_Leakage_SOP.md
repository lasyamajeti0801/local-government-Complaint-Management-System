# WATER SUPPLY PIPELINE MAINTENANCE & LEAKAGE RECTIFICATION SOP
**Document ID:** DOC-WTR-2026-003  
**Authority:** Department of Water Supply & Distribution Engineering  
**Effective Date:** 01 February 2026  
**Classification:** INTERNAL_OFFICER (DEPARTMENT OFFICERS & FIELD ENGINEERS ONLY)

## SECTION 1: ISOLATION PROTOCOLS AND SLUICE VALVE CONTROL

1.1 Upon receipt of an NGC high-priority ticket reporting main line rupture or significant pressure loss, the Nodal Water Officer must inspect the GIS hydraulic network map within 60 minutes.
1.2 Upstream and downstream sluice valves must be shut gradually to prevent sudden water hammer shocks that can crack adjacent cast iron (CI) or ductile iron (DI) joints.
1.3 In the event of an isolation lasting longer than 4 hours during scheduled supply windows, the officer must notify the Ward Corporator and mobilize at least two 5,000-liter drinking water tankers for the affected sector.

---page-break---

## SECTION 2: LEAK REPAIR METHODOLOGIES AND EXCAVATION SAFETY

2.1 For pinhole leakages or radial hairline cracks in ductile iron pipes:
* Apply high-tensile stainless steel repair clamp sleeves with EPDM elastomeric gaskets.
* Torque bolts to 65 Nm using calibrated torque wrenches.
2.2 For longitudinal bursts exceeding 300 mm:
* Cut damaged pipe section using hydraulic mechanical pipe cutters.
* Install spool piece coupled with universal mechanical slip couplings.
2.3 Trench excavation exceeding 1.2 meters depth requires mandatory aluminum trench shoring boxes to prevent side collapse.

---page-break---

## SECTION 3: RESIDUAL CHLORINE VERIFICATION AND WATER QUALITY RE-ENTRY

3.1 Following pipe rectification, flush pipeline thoroughly using scouring hydrants until turbidity drops below 5 NTU.
3.2 Dosing with sodium hypochlorite is mandatory to ensure free residual chlorine level of at least 0.2 mg/L (ppm) at consumer tail-ends.
3.3 Field staff must record digital photo of chlorine test kit readout and upload it as mandatory resolution evidence in Nagar Connect before closing the ticket.
