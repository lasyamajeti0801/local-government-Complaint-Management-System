# MUNICIPAL CORPORATION OF NAGAR - STATUTORY CODE
## ROAD EXCAVATION AND RIGHT OF WAY RESTORATION RULES
**Statutory Code:** NMC-LAW-2026-005
**Promulgation Authority:** Department of Municipal Administration & Urban Development
**Validity:** 01 January 2026 through 31 December 2030

---page-break---

### SECTION 1: STATUTORY CLAUSE AND REGULATORY MANDATE 1

**Clause 1.1 [Jurisdiction and Legal Mandate]:**
In exercise of powers conferred under Section 438 of the State Municipalities Act, 1965, the Municipal Corporation hereby establishes statutory compliance protocols governing civic asset maintenance, public grievance tracking, and technical SLA accountability across all 150 administrative wards of the Nagar Municipal Corporation.

**Clause 1.2 [Technical Inspection and Enforcement]:**
Departmental Officers, Junior Engineers, and Ward Sanitation Inspectors are designated as authorized reporting officers under this code. Any deviation detected during periodic bi-weekly field surveys shall be logged automatically into the Nagar Connect central grievance database with high-resolution photographic evidence and GPS coordinates.

**Clause 1.3 [Compounding Penalty & Rectification SLA]:**
The designated contractor or concessionaire responsible for maintenance failure under Section 1 shall rectify defects within 13 hours of computerized alert dispatch. Failure to rectify within the statutory window shall trigger automatic daily liquidated damages of ₹ 2,500 per day.
